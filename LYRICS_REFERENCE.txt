Manual of Youth — school lyric-sheet practice order (corrected).
The first 32 lines match the school minus-one; the remaining 21 are for Sung.
All Chinese lines, Pinyin and English below come from the supplied lyric material.
Note: chorus 3 is lines 33–40; bridge/rap begins at line 41.

Chorus 1 — lines 1–8
跟着我左手右手一个慢动作
gēn zhe wǒ zuǒ shǒu yòu shǒu yī gè màn dòng zuò
Follow my left hand and the right in slow motion

右手左手慢动作重播
yòu shǒu zuǒ shǒu màn dòng zuò chóng bō
Right hand, left hand, slow motion reply

这首歌给你快乐
zhè shǒu gē gěi nǐ kuài lè
The song makes you happy

你有没有爱上我
nǐ yǒu méi yǒu ài shàng wǒ
Have you fallen in love with me?

跟着我鼻子眼睛动一动耳朵
gēn zhe wǒ bí zi yǎn jīng dòng yí dòng ěr duo
Follow my nose and my eyes and move your ears

装乖耍帅换不停风格
zhuāng guāi shuǎ shuài huàn bù tíng fēng gé
Be cute or stay cool, change different styles

青春有太多未知的猜测
qīng chūn yǒu tài duō wèi zhī de cāi cè
Youth is full of uncertainties

成长的烦恼算什么
chéng zhǎng de fán nǎo suàn shén me
Take it easy with the growing pains 1,2,3 Go


Verse 1 — lines 9–12
皮鞋擦亮换上西装
pí xié cā liàng huàn shàng xī zhuāng
Polish your shoes, wear a suit

佩戴上一克拉的梦想
pèi dài shàng yī kè lā de mèng xiǎng
Carry one-carat dream

我的勇敢充满电量
wǒ de yǒng gǎn chōng mǎn diàn liàng
I’m fully charged with courage

昂首到达每一个地方
áng shǒu dào dá měi yí gè dì fāng
It takes me anywhere I want


Pre-Chorus 1 — lines 13–16
这世界的太阳
zhè shì jiè de tài yáng
The sun of the world

因为自信才能把我照亮
yīn wèi zì xìn cái néng bǎ wǒ zhào liàng
Shines on my confidence

这舞台的中央
zhè wǔ tái de zhōng yāng
The centre of the stage

有我才闪亮有我才能发着光
yǒu wǒ cái shǎn liàng yǒu wǒ cái néng fā zhe guāng
I’m the reason for the spotlight

Verse 2 — lines 25–28
经常会想长大多好
jīng cháng huì xiǎng zhǎng dà duō hǎo
Always think it’s so good to grow up

有些事情却只能想象
yǒu xiē shì qíng què zhǐ néng xiǎng xiàng

想说就说想做就做
xiǎng shuō jiù shuō xiǎng zuò jiù zuò
Say what you want to say, do what you want to do

wèi le míng tiān de zì jǐ gǔ zhǎng

Bridge / Rap — lines 41–45
向明天对不起
xiàng míng tiān duì bù qǐ
Face tomorrow [I’m Sorry]

向前冲不客气
xiàng qián chōng bù kè qi
Run ahead [You’re welcome]

一路有你充满斗志无限动力
yí lù yǒu nǐ chōng mǎn dòu zhì wú xiàn dòng lì
All the journey together with you, I’m full of energy

男子汉没有什么输不起
nán zǐ hàn méi yǒu shén me shū bù qǐ
Boys don’t fear any failures

正太修炼成功的秘籍
zhèng tài xiū liàn chéng gōng de mì jí
This is the secret manual to success
